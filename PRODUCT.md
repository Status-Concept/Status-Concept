# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Homeowners, designers, and hospitality clients furnishing outdoor spaces across the Algarve, alongside the showroom team supporting product discovery and enquiries.

## Product Purpose

Status Concept is a premium outdoor-furniture catalogue and showroom-led enquiry site. It helps people browse furniture, shade, kitchens, and related outdoor-living pieces, then move toward an in-person showroom visit or proposal request.

## Brand Personality

Quietly luxurious, material-conscious, warm, and service-first. The experience should feel confident and considered without being loud or sales-heavy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic ecommerce urgency, dense discount-led merchandising, noisy promotional banners, and overly decorative luxury styling that obscures product details.

## Design Principles

- Show the materials and settings clearly so clients can judge pieces with confidence.
- Keep discovery calm, curated, and easy to scan.
- Make showroom visits and human help feel like natural next steps.
- Treat product names and specifications as precise source data.
- Keep the experience useful in both English and Portuguese.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic navigation, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, meaningful image alternatives, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Maintain readable contrast and avoid relying on color alone for state.
