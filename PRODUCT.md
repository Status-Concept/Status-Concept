# STATVS Website

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are homeowners, property managers, architects and interior
designers furnishing premium outdoor spaces in the Algarve. They arrive looking
for confidence in quality, suitability for the coastal climate and a direct
route to seeing products in person.

## Product Purpose

The website is the digital front door for STATVS. It should establish trust,
show the breadth of the outdoor-living offer and turn interest into showroom
visits or qualified enquiries. Success means visitors understand the brand,
recognise the quality of the offer and know what to do next.

## Brand Personality

Calm, tactile and assured.

The voice is understated, specific and service-led. It avoids hype and lets
materials, installed settings and long-term care demonstrate quality.

## Anti-references

- Generic luxury templates built from beige cards and vague superlatives
- Loud sale-led furniture retail websites
- Fashion-editorial styling that obscures products or practical information
- Technology-dashboard patterns, glass effects and decorative gradients
- Claims, project names or imagery that cannot be verified by STATVS

## Design Principles

1. Show the real object. Product, material and showroom imagery carries trust.
2. Make the showroom the natural next step. High-consideration purchases need
   physical reassurance.
3. Be quietly specific. Use useful facts about materials, climate performance,
   delivery and care instead of luxury cliches.
4. Let space communicate confidence. Keep layouts composed, readable and free
   from unnecessary interface furniture.
5. Continue the relationship after delivery. After Care is a core brand
   difference, not a secondary service.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All primary journeys must work with keyboard navigation,
visible focus states, reduced motion, semantic landmarks, descriptive image
text and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels. English and Portuguese
experiences should carry equivalent meaning and functionality.
