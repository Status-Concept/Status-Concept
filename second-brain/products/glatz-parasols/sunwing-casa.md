# SUNWING® Casa — The handy all-round parasol

**Category:** Side-mast (cantilever) · Garden parasol

Always there for you when you need shade — but never in your way. The canopy
floats freely above you; one crank opens it in the blink of an eye, another
tilts it steplessly up to 45°.

## Specs
- Crank drive for opening/closing; second crank for **stepless 45° tilt** both directions
- **360° rotation**; self-tensioning canopy via flexible rib ends
- Frame: 8 ribs, aluminium; round mast 55×2.5 mm
- Die-cast aluminium crank housing, support arm guide, head; stainless fittings
- Int. design registered

## Sizes
Round Ø300 / Ø330 cm · Square 270×270 cm · Rectangular 300×240 cm
