- Full original accessory ecosystem: bases, ground sockets, wall consoles, heaters, lighting, protective covers, rain gutters + original spare parts for every model

## Fabric system (70 colours, 3 quality classes)

Colour collections: **"Morning Light"** and **"Sunset Glow"** — 70 colours total.

| Property | Class 5 (recommended) | Class 4 | Class 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Material | 100% polyacrylic | 100% polyester staple fibre | 100% polyester |
| Grammage | 300 g/m² | 250 g/m² | 220 g/m² |
| Dyeing | Spun-dyed | Spun-dyed | Piece-dyed |
| Lightfastness | 350–700 sunny days (level 7–8) | min. 350 days (level 7) | 80–160 days (level 5–6) |
| Water column | >350 mm | >150 mm | >100 mm |
| UV protection | >98% | >98% | >98% |

- Class 5 = awning-grade fabric, matt and firm look, dirt/rot-inhibiting coating — Glatz's recommendation
- Spun-dyed = colour pigments reach the fibre core (classes 4 & 5); piece-dyed = dye coats the thread only (class 2)
- All classes are water-repellent and SPF-50-equivalent

## Fastening guidance (useful for advice content / after-care upsell)

- **Fixed spot on a terrace** → concreted-in ground socket (no base to trip over; ideal for hospitality)
- **Following the sun during the day** → base with wheels
- **Mostly stationary, not blocking walkways** → standard base (various sizes/weights/materials)
- Glatz performance promises (wind resistance, warranty) only guaranteed with original fastening accessories — good argument for selling original bases alongside parasols
