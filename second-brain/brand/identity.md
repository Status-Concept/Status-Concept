## Positioning
"A quieter kind of luxury" — premium outdoor furniture for the most distinguished residences across the Algarve.
Serves: Vale do Lobo, Quinta do Lago, Vilamoura, Almancil, Tavira, and beyond.

## Brand Pillars / Values (live on About page)
1. **Material Honesty** — Every fabric, frame and finish is chosen because it performs outdoors, not for looks alone
2. **Showroom-Led** — Clients see scale, comfort and material quality in person before choosing
3. **Curated Suppliers** — Premium European manufacturers, thoughtful materials
4. **After Care** — Service culture that continues after delivery; seasonal valet and furniture care
5. **Outdoor Living Expertise** — Covers furniture, shade, kitchens, decor and project support
